Export and backup

Reviews save automatically in this browser as you tap. They stay on this phone only, so export or back up at the end of each field day. Clearing the browser’s site data deletes them.

The road map needs a data connection; the segments and your reviews work without one once the page has loaded.

Segment lines come from OakDOT’s Paving_PCI layer (PCI 2024 from the City’s latest survey). Rows the City layer couldn’t match exactly carry a yellow note on the segment.
